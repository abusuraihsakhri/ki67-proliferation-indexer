Interpretation and limitations

The index is a descriptive measurement, not a standalone tumour grade, diagnosis, prognosis or treatment recommendation. Counting area, sampling method, cell eligibility and grade cutoffs depend on the tumour type and reporting protocol. This tool does not analyze microscopy images or implement the IKWG weighted-global scoring application.

Scientific reference: International Ki67 Working Group recommendations (JNCI, 2021).
